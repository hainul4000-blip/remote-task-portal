Privacy policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy describes how RemoteTaskPortal (“we”) handles information when you visit this website. Third-party offer providers have their own privacy policies, which apply when you visit their services.

Information you provide

If you contact us, we receive the information you include, such as your email address and message. We use it to respond and maintain the directory.

Basic technical information

Our hosting provider and security services may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, and request logs to deliver and protect the website. We do not operate task tracking or completion verification.

Analytics

This site loads Histats.com analytics on pages that run JavaScript. Histats may process visit and technical information, and may use cookies or similar technologies, under its own policies. You can block third-party scripts in your browser settings.

Cookies and third-party links

This site is designed to function without advertising cookies from us. A provider you visit may use cookies or similar technologies under its own policy. Outbound links may contain provider or affiliate tracking parameters.

How we use information

We use contact messages to reply, correct listings, and maintain the site. We do not sell contact details. We may retain messages as reasonably needed for those purposes and applicable obligations.

Your choices

You can choose not to send us personal information. To ask about a message you sent, contact hello@remotetaskportal.site.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the site changes. The date above shows the latest revision. For privacy questions, email hello@remotetaskportal.site.

Third-party services: Once you follow an offer link, the provider’s privacy terms and practices apply. Review them before sharing information.
