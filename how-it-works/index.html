How it works

Clear steps. Your choice.

We help you discover third-party offers and understand what to check before you continue.

1. Browse by country

Choose Indonesia, Malaysia, or the Philippines to see offers currently listed for that location. Availability can change, and a listing does not guarantee that you will qualify.

2. Review the offer

Read the description and follow the provider’s own information about eligibility, time, reward conditions, privacy, and any other requirements. If a detail is unclear, ask the provider before signing up.

3. Continue directly to the provider

When an offer is available, its button takes you to the provider’s page. The provider operates the activity and is responsible for its sign-up, terms, rewards, and support.

4. Contact the provider about participation

RemoteTaskPortal does not track task completion, approve rewards, or handle provider accounts. Use the provider’s support channel for questions about an offer you joined.

About rewards

Rewards are not guaranteed. Amounts, eligibility, and payment conditions are determined by the third-party provider and may change.

Before you start

  • Confirm the provider and its terms.
  • Never pay a fee unless you have independently verified why it is required.
  • Do not share sensitive information unless you trust the provider and understand its privacy terms.
